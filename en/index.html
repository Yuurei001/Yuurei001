01 · About

Connecting the shop floor to business systems

I hold a B.Sc. in Information Technology (Very Good classification) from Thai Nguyen University of Information and Communication Technology, 2025. I completed the Samsung Innovation Campus Artificial Intelligence course and co-authored a paper on YOLOv8-based emergency detection published by Springer.

I started my career as a test engineer at Fuyu Precision Component, managing 25 Keysight 3070 in-circuit testers across 4 production lines. I then moved on to developing and maintaining the ERP, MES and HRM systems at INTOPS Vietnam.

Having worked on the shop floor, I understand where production data comes from and how it needs to be presented to support decisions. I am focused on growing in manufacturing execution systems (MES) and factory digital transformation.
